Why your dashboard lies, and what a diff can do about it

By Priya Natarajan · November 3, 2026

Every dashboard we shipped looked right on the day it shipped. The trouble started the week after, when a font fell back, a chart library rounded differently, and a card quietly grew eight pixels taller. Nobody noticed, because nobody looks at the same screen twice.

A screenshot is a promise about what the user saw. A diff is how you find out whether you kept it.

Pixel comparison catches all of it, and then some. The craft is in choosing which differences matter.

Dusk over the valley. Gradients and soft edges are where JPEG shows its seams.
