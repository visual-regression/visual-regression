## The short version

The site sets no cookies, loads nothing from other companies, and has no forms or accounts. The viewer works inside your browser: the files you drop are not uploaded.

## What happens when you visit

| What | Why | Basis | Who | How long |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Your IP address, browser, and the page you request, handled by the hosting network when it serves the page | To deliver the site and keep it secure | Legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR) | Cloudflare (hosting on Cloudflare Pages) | Cloudflare's log retention; we keep no logs of our own |

We do not run analytics on this site. If that changes, we update this notice before it goes live.

## Items stored in your browser

These stay on your device. They are not sent to us and are not used to track you.

| Item (local storage) | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| `nuxt-color-mode` | Remembers your light or dark theme choice |
| `content_collection_docs`, `content_checksum_docs` | Keeps a local copy of the site's pages so they load faster |
| `assistant-messages`, `assistant-open` | Created by the site's theme for an assistant feature that is switched off; they hold empty values |

Because these are needed to show the site you asked for, or record a choice you made, they need no consent banner. You can clear them any time in your browser settings.

## The viewer

The viewer at [/viewer](/viewer) reads the manifest and images you choose, in your browser, and shows them to you. The files are not uploaded and are not sent anywhere.

## Links, source code and other sites

The site links to GitHub (the project's source, issues and discussions), npm and tool websites. They are separate and have their own privacy policies. If you open an issue or comment on GitHub, GitHub's policy applies to that.
