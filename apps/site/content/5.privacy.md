## Items stored in your browser

These stay on your device. They are not sent to us and are not used to track you.

| Item (local storage) | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| `nuxt-color-mode` | Remembers your light or dark theme choice |
| `content_collection_docs`, `content_checksum_docs` | Keeps a local copy of the site's pages so they load faster |
| `assistant-messages`, `assistant-open` | Created by the site's theme for an assistant feature that is switched off; they hold empty values |

Because these are needed to show the site you asked for, or record a choice you made, they need no consent banner. You can clear them any time in your browser settings.

## The viewer

The viewer at [/viewer](/viewer) reads the manifest and images you choose, in your browser, and shows them to you. The files are not uploaded and are not sent anywhere. If you give the viewer the address of a zip (the field on the page, or `?zip=` or `#zip=` in the page address), your browser fetches that address directly from the server it names, with no cookies or credentials, and reads the zip locally. We do not see the zip. A `?zip=` address is part of the page request, so the hosting network handles it like any other requested page address; use `#zip=` for an address that carries a secret, because the part after `#` is never sent to a server. The `visual-regression-viewer` command line tool runs on your machine only: it makes no network requests and sends no telemetry.
